DigiKey list prices in USD, for the quantity you need (price breaks and minimum orders included). Searches are guesses from the circuit alone: it has no package, tolerance, voltage rating or part number. Parts tagged “assumed” are generic stand-ins. “Next choice” means nothing acceptable matched a higher-ranked type from your settings. With a brand ranking saved, the top pick is the cheapest option from your most-preferred brand group, not necessarily the cheapest overall. Edit any search and press Enter to re-check that line. The cheapest option is picked from DigiKey's top matches, so always confirm the part before ordering.

Common-value alternatives

Resistors and capacitors that aren't common stock values can often be built from a series or parallel pair of common ones. Networks made from values already on your list are shown first, since reusing a value saves ordering another one. Your parts list isn't changed.

PCB (KiCad 9)

Creates a KiCad project with a schematic ready for layout. You choose the ICs for the op-amps, every DC rail of the same voltage is joined into one net, and a single power connector brings in all the supplies. You then place and route the board in KiCad (Update PCB from Schematic), and order from there.
